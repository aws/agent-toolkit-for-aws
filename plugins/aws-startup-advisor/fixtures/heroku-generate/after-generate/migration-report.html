Timeline

Shape: Full cutover: one migration window. This is a stage order with its duration drivers, not a staffed schedule and not a commitment.

  1. Provision the AWS environment from the generated Terraform (account baseline first, then the Beanstalk environment and the RDS instance).
  2. Migrate the database with dump and restore inside the migration window.
  3. Cut over every app in that same window with weighted Route 53 records, then decommission the Heroku app.

Duration drivers: a small estate (two services, none deferred), a Multi-AZ database migrated by dump and restore (20 GB estimated), and a maintenance window of Sunday at 04:00 UTC for database patching. The cutover and rollback procedure stays in MIGRATION_GUIDE.md.

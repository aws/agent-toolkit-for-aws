## Drata for AWS Startups: 25% off your first contract

### Estimated offer value: $11,250.00

**Offer:** Eligible AWS Activate startups receive 25% off their first Drata contract.

**What's included:**

- Automated evidence collection
- Continuous control monitoring
- One pre-mapped compliance framework to help teams get started quickly
- A customizable Trust Center for sharing current security and compliance information with customers
- Onboarding support from Drata compliance experts

**Eligibility:** New Drata customers that are AWS Activate members with up to 50 employees, based in North America (United States, Canada, Mexico).

**Offer period:** Available through September 28, 2027.

**How to redeem:** Apply through this page and Drata will reach out to set up your discounted first contract.

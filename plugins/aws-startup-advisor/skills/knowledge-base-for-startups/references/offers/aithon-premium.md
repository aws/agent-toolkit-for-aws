## Aithon Premium | Up to $20K AWS credits + 50% off the full platform

### Estimated offer value: $23,000.00

Aithon: AI that helps AWS startups find customers and win deals. Spot new customers that are ready to buy, and close them on AWS - with funding and budget.

**What's included:**

- Prospecting: find new accounts, find the right people to talk to with verified email and phone, and use Aithon's intelligence to send custom emails and LinkedIn outreach
- Deal Intelligence: see which deals are at risk, what to do next, and who is involved on the buyer's side
- AWS: manage all aspects of your partnership from Aithon, including AWS Marketplace listing management and co-selling with AWS (seller engagement, funding, private offers)
- Hands-on onboarding

**Pricing:** $1,000/month for months 1-3 (50% off the $2,000 list price), then $2,000/month.

**Credits:** Aithon can unlock up to $20,000 in AWS credits. Aithon handles the application. Subject to AWS eligibility and approval.

**Eligibility:** AWS Activate members based in the US or Canada. Seed stage or later B2B startups. New Aithon customers only.

**How to redeem:** Apply, take a 15-minute call to confirm it's a fit, and get a custom offer through AWS Marketplace.
